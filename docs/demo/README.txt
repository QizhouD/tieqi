繁體中文

【這是什麼】
以明軍遼東騎兵為主視角的壬辰戰爭戰術遊戲試玩版。場景是 1593 年的碧蹄館之戰：可暫停的即時制，衝鋒破陣、士氣崩潰、潰兵散開與收攏。

【執行】
1. 把壓縮檔整個解壓縮到任意資料夾（不要直接在壓縮檔裡執行）。
2. 雙擊 tieqi.exe。
不需要另外安裝任何執行階段或驅動以外的元件。需要 Windows 10 或 11（64 位元），以及支援 OpenGL 的顯示卡。

【操作】
- 點選棋子：選中我軍；Shift 多選，拖動框選，A 全選可戰部隊。
- 點地面：移動；點敵軍即進攻，距離夠會自動衝鋒。右鍵也可以下令。
- 懸停敵軍：右側顯示衝擊預估和全部修正項。
- 快捷鍵：空格 開戰／暫停，1 2 3 變速，H 堅守，R 撤往北口，F 分兵，G 合兵，Esc 取消，F5 全螢幕。
- 暫停時也可以下令。開局畫面有騎兵要訣，建議先讀完再開戰。

【設定】
右上角「設定」可以切換語言（繁體中文、English、한국어）和「減少動態」（關掉震屏、縮短頓幀）。設定存在 tieqi.exe 旁邊的 settings.txt；如果資料夾不能寫入，下次啟動會回到預設值。

【已知事項】
- 兵種數值與地形、兵力都是暫定，尚待對照史料核實。
- 英文與韓文是初稿，正在審校。
- 音效是程式即時合成的暫代音。
- 沒有存檔，只保存設定。

【問題回報】
請附上版本號 @VERSION@、作業系統版本、顯示卡型號，以及問題出現前做了什麼。

ABOUT
A tactics demo set in the Imjin War, played mainly from the side of the Ming army's Liaodong cavalry. The scenario is the Battle of Byeokjegwan, 1593: pausable real time, cavalry charges that break formations, morale collapse, and routed units that scatter and regroup.

RUNNING
1. Extract the whole zip to any folder (do not run it from inside the zip).
2. Double-click tieqi.exe.
Nothing else needs to be installed beyond your graphics driver. Requires Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and a graphics card with OpenGL support.

CONTROLS
- Click a piece: select your unit; Shift to add, drag to box-select, A selects every unit able to fight.
- Click the ground: move; click an enemy to attack (units charge when close enough). Right-click works too.
- Hover an enemy: the right panel shows the charge estimate and every modifier.
- Keys: Space begin/pause, 1 2 3 speed, H hold, R retreat to the north exit, F split, G join, Esc cancel, F5 fullscreen.
- You can give orders while paused. The opening screen explains cavalry essentials; read it before you begin.

SETTINGS
"Settings" at the top right switches the language (繁體中文, English, 한국어) and "Reduce motion" (no screen shake, shorter hit-stop). Settings are saved to settings.txt next to tieqi.exe; if the folder is not writable, the defaults return on the next start.

KNOWN ISSUES
- Unit values, terrain, and troop numbers are provisional and still to be checked against historical sources.
- The English and Korean text is a first draft under review.
- Sound effects are placeholders synthesized at run time.
- There are no saved games; only settings are kept.

REPORTING PROBLEMS
Please include the version (@VERSION@), your Windows version, your graphics card, and what you did before the problem appeared.
